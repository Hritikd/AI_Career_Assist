# Daystep | Communication and engagement plan

| Segment and stage | Trigger and channel | Timing and frequency | Candidate-facing copy | Suppression and outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Exploring jobs | Two jobs saved, inline prompt on Jobs or My shortlist | On second save while both remain shortlisted | “You saved two openings with different shift patterns. Want to compare them?” | Dismiss persists on device. Measure completed decisions per eligible visitor. |
| Preparing to apply | User opens a specific job, inline rehearsal button | On demand, on that listing | “Rehearse for this job” | User initiates. Measure finished rehearsal and meaningful revision. |
| Weighing training | Learning comparison, inline prompt | While browsing free and paid alternatives | “Unsure whether you need a paid course? Compare it with the free dispatch exercise.” | Suppress after dismissal. Measure uptake by budget, free exercise completion and qualified paid interest. |
| Committing to an action | Mira suggests a step in the conversation | Immediately, editable and explicitly saved | “Save this step” | A suggestion alone never counts as a saved action. Measure save and completion separately. |
| Returning on the same device | Previous transcript and chosen action, in app | At next voluntary visit | The user's saved action appears with “Mark complete.” | Completed actions show “Completed.” Track time to completion and voluntary return. |
| Opted in with unfinished rehearsal | Proposed push or WhatsApp in chosen language | One message after 24 hours, daytime only, maximum one per seven days | “Want to finish your dispatch rehearsal? Your answer is saved. Continue when you have five minutes.” | Cancel on completion, dismissal, opt-out or stale listing. Use one channel per action. Measure incremental completions and opt-outs against holdout. |
| Wants spoken rehearsal away from the screen | Optional Bolna call, only on explicit request | One requested call, no automatic redial | “Would you like a short practice call about this role?” | Only a verified consenting recipient in the trial. Track completed rehearsals, abandoned calls and cost per completed rehearsal. |

**Measurement plan.** Instrument listing_saved, comparison_offered, conversation_started, rehearsal_answered, revision_submitted, action_saved and action_completed. Count each user once per eligible opportunity. Segment by language, entry point and new versus returning. Review transcription corrections and unsupported listing claims. Lock-screen copy should be generic unless detailed previews are enabled. External reminders and analytics are pilot plans. The demo implements the in-app journey and local persistence.
